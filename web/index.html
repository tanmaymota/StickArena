Controls

  • AD Player 1 move
  • ←→ Player 2 move
  • W / Space Player 1 jump
  • ↑ Player 2 jump
  • F / O Attack
  • S / ↓ Block
  • G / P Special
  • Esc Pause

About this browser version

Stick Arena is a student-made Godot project. The game runs on your device inside the browser; this site does not add online multiplayer. For sound, click or press a key once after the game loads if your browser keeps audio muted.
